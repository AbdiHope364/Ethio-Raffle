1. Platform Identity (System Provider Model)
Who We Are: Ethio-Raffle is strictly a Technology Infrastructure & Escrow Provider. The platform owns zero physical inventory. We provide a trusted, licensed bridge connecting independent merchants (Sellers) with ticket buyers, backed by automated escrow protection and statutory government tax deduction.

3. 100% Anti-Cheat Guarantee (SHA-256 Explained Simply)

To make it mathematically impossible for admins or sellers to rig the draw, the system uses a "Digital Wax Seal":

Step 1 Sealed in Advance
Before ticket #1 is sold, a secret key is locked into a public SHA-256 fingerprint (e.g. c9a9d30d...).
Step 2 Tamper-Proof Sales
Tickets #1 to #10,000 are sold. Changing even 1 letter in the secret completely changes the hash, exposing any cheat.
Step 3 Midnight Draw (12:00 AM)
When 100% sold out, the computer calculates the winner using pure math: (Seed + Tickets) % Total = Winner.
Step 4 Public 1-Click Verification
Anyone can test the draw on the /verifier tool to confirm the math was 100% honest.
4. Winner Settlement & Handover Protection
Stage What Happens Security & Financial Safeguard
1. Winner Notification Winner receives SMS notification and accesses settlement console on /my-tickets. Verified phone number authentication gate.
2. Choice of Prize Option 1: Physical Item (Car/Phone/Condo)
Option 2: Cash Equivalent credited to wallet.		 If Cash is chosen, funds credit instantly to wallet; item stays in merchant stock.
3. Physical QR Handover Winner presents Dynamic Claim QR Code to the merchant during physical pickup. Merchant scans QR $\rightarrow$ Escrow balance instantly unfreezes into merchant payout balance.
4. 7-Day Safety Fallback If QR scan is not completed, system auto-releases funds to merchant after 7 days. If buyer reports non-receipt, escrow is permanently frozen for Admin Arbitration.
