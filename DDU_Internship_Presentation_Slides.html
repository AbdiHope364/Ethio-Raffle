Slide 4 of 12 Problem Statement

Problem Statement: Traditional Raffle Bottlenecks

  • Opaque Manual Draws: Physical paper lotteries lack digital transparency and verifiable mathematical integrity.
  • Database Concurrency Overselling: High-traffic sales cause race conditions where seats/tickets sell beyond available inventory.
  • Escrow Delivery Disputes: Winner prize disbursements often lack dynamic cryptographic proof of handover.
  • Manual Tax Reconciliation: Difficulty in calculating and remitting statutory 15% VAT to the Ministry of Revenues (MoR).
Speaker Note: "These 4 core bottlenecks in Ethiopia's commercial lottery ecosystem defined the technical requirements for our platform."

Slide 9 of 12 Key Deliverables

Double-Entry Financial Ledger Implementation

  • Every ticket sale atomically posts balanced journal entries across standard Chart of Accounts:
  • Debit: 1010-CASH-TRANSIT (Asset • 100%)
  • Credit: 2020-VAT-PAYABLE (Liability • 15% MoR Tax Escrow)
  • Credit: 2010-PRIZE-ESCROW (Liability • 77% Seller Holding)
  • Credit: 4010-PLATFORM-REVENUE (Revenue • 8% Platform Fee)
  • Trial Balance Status: 100% Balanced with zero mathematical variance (Δ = 0.00).
Speaker Note: "This guarantees that money in transit, prize escrow, and government taxes are balanced down to the exact cent."
Slide 10 of 12 Cryptographic Integrity

Provably Fair Multi-Entropy RNG & Snapshot Lock

  • Pre-Commitment: Digital SHA-256 commitment published before selling ticket #1.
  • Immutable Snapshot: When sales close, the exact ticket universe is locked in DrawSnapshot.
  • Two-Person Consensus: Draw Operator initiates → Super Admin approves.
  • Deterministic Math: Winner = (BigInt(SHA256(v2.0 : RaffleID : Seed : Sold : PublicEntropy : Algo)) % Sold) + 1.
  • Public Verifier: Instant 1-click independent proof validator at /verifier.
Speaker Note: "Neither the operator nor the buyer can manipulate the winning outcome once the pre-commitment is published."
