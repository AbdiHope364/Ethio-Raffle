5. Introduction

The Software Engineering Internship (SEng-4182) at Dire Dawa University is a cornerstone practical course designed to immerse students into enterprise software development workflows. The attachment allows students to apply theoretical knowledge from courses like Database Systems, Web Engineering, Software Architecture, and Computer Security directly to commercial systems.

During my 4-month attachment with LuckyEthio Digital Solutions PLC, I was embedded within the core engineering team responsible for architecting and building high-traffic, secure, and regulatory-compliant digital ticketing and financial settlement systems.

6. Host Company Profile: LuckyEthio Digital Solutions PLC

LuckyEthio is a specialized Ethiopian technology startup focused on revolutionizing traditional promotional raffles, commercial lotteries, and point-of-sale ticketing. By replacing physical paper slips with cryptographically verifiable digital receipts, automated 15% VAT withholding, and instant mobile money settlements (Telebirr, CBE Birr, Chapa), LuckyEthio bridges digital convenience with strict regulatory transparency.

Organizational Hierarchy: The engineering division comprises the Chief Technology Officer, Frontend UI/UX Team, Core Systems & Backend Team (where I served as Software Engineering Intern), Quality Assurance, and Regulatory Compliance Officers.

7. Internship Activities & Roles

My primary responsibilities centered around full-stack feature engineering, database architecture, and cryptographic protocol implementation:

  • Monorepo Engineering: Restructured the codebase into a modular Turborepo containing apps/web, apps/admin, packages/database, and packages/shared.
  • Double-Entry Financial Ledger: Engineered balanced ledger transactions (Debits = Credits) for ticket receipts, 15% VAT escrow, seller holdings, and agent commissions.
  • Atomic Ticket Concurrency: Implemented database-level transaction locks (tx.$transaction) and composite unique constraints to guarantee zero overselling under high concurrency.
  • Multi-Provider Payment Adapters: Built unified provider adapters for Chapa, Telebirr, CBE Birr, and SantimPay with idempotent webhook handlers.
  • Provably Fair RNG Engine: Designed a multi-entropy commit-reveal formula with immutable DrawSnapshot state locks.
  • Data Privacy Governance: Implemented customer data rights workflows adhering to Ethiopian PDPP Proclamation No. 1321/2024.
